Singapore Rail, Drawn to Scale

The official MRT map is a diagram. Here every line and station sits where it really is on the island, so you can see what the diagram bends.

Accurate for routing

Station order, line colours and interchanges on the official map are correct. Use it to plan a journey.

Not accurate as geography

It is not to scale. Gaps between stations range 0.4–4.2 km in reality but are drawn nearly equal, and lines are straightened to 45° angles.

Each station is scored by how quickly it reaches the places you choose. Add any stations as destinations and give each a weight; heavier ones count more. You can also tap a station on the map and add it from its card. A hub reached in a few minutes counts almost fully; one an hour away counts for little. Travel time includes the train ride, a 5-minute penalty per change of line, and short walks to stations under 600 m away. Scores run 0–100, relative to the best-connected station.
